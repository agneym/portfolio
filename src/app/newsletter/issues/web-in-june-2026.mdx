## Releases

### Browsers

### [Chrome 150](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/150)

Chrome had two releases in June - 149 and 150. Putting 150 in the headline, because well.. it's 150.

- `rect` and `xywh` functions in `shape-outside` property land in Chrome and baseline newly available
- automatic text scaling with `text-fit`
- CSS gap decorations in flex and grid layouts
- `background-clip: border-area`
- scroll to functions return a promise so you can run follow up tasks after completing the scroll
- declarative keyboard navigation with `focusgroup`
- animatable zoom property

### [Firefox 152](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Mozilla/Firefox/Releases/152)

- `field-sizing` property is on Firefox and baseline newly available
- `AnimationEvent.animation` and `TransitionEvent.animation` properties are now supported
- Notification actions are supported

## In the Spotlight 🔦

### Rise of StyleX

June was the month for StyleX, seemingly for no apparent reason. I noticed prominent individuals and companies discussing their migration to StyleX from Tailwind due to its superior type safety experience. It's worth noting that StyleX has been around for quite some time.

- Polar has a [blog post](https://polar.sh/blog/orbit-llm-safe-design-system) about building a design system with StyleX and their assumptions for it being agent-ready.
- We also heard Linear is migrating from Styled components to StyleX for their design system.
- StyleX is keeping a list of companies already migrated/launched with it

[https://x.com/stylexjs/status/2068834315870638158](https://x.com/stylexjs/status/2068834315870638158)

- Meta released an open source design system components named [astryx](https://astryx.atmeta.com/) built on StyleX

### Loops 🔁

June was undoubtedly the month of loop engineering. Both OpenAI and Anthropic pledged their allegence to loop engineering and stated that we should avoid modifying codebases while building a software factory. It's not a viewpoint that I would wish upon anyone who doesn't have unlimited tokens available to them.

- [Loop Engineering](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) - Addy Osmani
- [The Art of Loop Engineering](https://www.langchain.com/blog/the-art-of-loop-engineering) - Langchain

While you can choose your stance on loops, I believe that adding one or more verification steps will help the agent perform better on the tasks you assign to it.

- [Verification loops](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2061900434722496604) - Claude Devs

## In Other News

### [Midjourney goes from generating cat images to full-body ultrasound scans - TheVerge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/952011/midjourney-medical-ai-ultrasound-scan)

Midjourney, the AI image generator tool, asked people to guess what they were developing and informed us that it was hardware. Many, including myself, speculated that they were creating a tool similar to the Rabbit R1 or possibly Meta glasses with image generation and editing capabilities. However, it turns out they were actually building full-body scanners.

### [How 100 hospitals switched to pen and paper to defeat a national cyber-attack - BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c4gyk756mzlo)

In February 2024, a massive cyber-attack struck Romania’s healthcare system. Criminals deployed a ransomware strain called BackMyData, which targeted a widely used medical software system named Hippocrates. The ransomware scrambled crucial patient data, administrative records, and logistics files, with the attackers demanding a ransom of in bitcoin.To halt the spread of infection, hospitals were asked to disconnect from the network.

### [Legibility of Effort](https://eieio.games/blog/legibility-of-effort/)

A significant part of our lives involves determining effort and duration. When we assemble something from Ikea, the effort we invest adds emotional value to the furniture. A gift received from someone, knowing they spent their nights working on it, tends to be valued more than a similar item purchased from a ready-made store, even if the ready-made store offers better functionality. This blog talks about how AI agents have transformed the legibility of effort in codebases.
