March is coming to an end and it seems like every month is one upping the other on disasters.

I'm not going to waste [talking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxAaO2rsdIs) [about](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54XLXg4fYsc) [flattening](https://www.who.int/health-topics/coronavirus) the [curve](https://www.washingtonpost.com/graphics/2020/world/corona-simulator/) [because](https://meltingasphalt.com/interactive/outbreak/) [there](https://twitter.com/marcelsalathe/status/1242430736944201730) are so [many](https://www.ft.com/coronavirus-latest) [people](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtN-goy9VOY) who are [doing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sHP0UIdZyI4) it [better](https://twitter.com/gregjames/status/1241838854891622401) than I can.

But let's talk about something that has left me wondering. The effects of this on the remote working landscape. Absolutely all of the IT service sector companies have gone remote now and it is said that it will help drive more companies remote after this crisis. Well, not so much.

- A lot of people are feeling negative with the current work scenario which not just puts them remote but also barrs them with social interaction.
- Companies and Managers who have no experience with remote are putting out [severe](https://twitter.com/benyt/status/1238555167932506116)
  restrictions on people and I have heard friends who are afraid to move from the computer screen for lunch.

I wonder if more people will associate remote work with the loneliness that they experience now and seek to move away from it. People have been trying to help though:

- [Cultivating Empathy in times of Coronavirus](https://www.gse.harvard.edu/news/uk/20/03/cultivating-empathy-coronavirus-crisis)
- DHH gave their [Remote: Office not Required](https://basecamp.com/books/remote) book for free
- Shopify have their employees [1000$](https://www.cnbc.com/2020/03/12/coronavirus-shopify-gives-employees-1000-stipend-to-work-from-home.html) to setup their home systems.
