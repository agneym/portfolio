## In the Spotlight 🔦

### Bun in Rust

[https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/pull/30412](https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/pull/30412)

Bun was rewritten from Zig to Rust, few things to note about this:

- The entire PR was done in a one week, in one single PR with _2188_ file changes. Almost entirely by AI, managing the test suite.

> Lines changed: **1009257** additions & **4024** deletions

- Claude team later revealed how this PR miracle was achieved in their post on [Claude dynamic workflows](https://claude.com/blog/introducing-dynamic-workflows-in-claude-code)
- Bun was built on Zig and is one of the flagship projects developed using this language. Zig, if you are not familiar, is designed to be an improved version of C. It operates as a non-profit corporation. The Zig and Bun teams have faced challenges after Bun's acquisition by Anthropic. Zig has been vocally against contributions from large language models (LLMs) and does not permit LLM involvement. While Bun has made many [improvements to its port of Zig](https://x.com/bunjavascript/status/2048427636414923250), it cannot contribute back due to the LLM policy.
